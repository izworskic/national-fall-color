How the decision is built

1. Historical transition at each milepost

USA-NPN MODIS Mid Green-down Median and historical variability are sampled independently at each Parkway anchor. This creates different timing windows instead of one regional date.

2. Current plants are evidence, not a percentage

Recent USA-NPN Nature’s Notebook “Colored leaves” observations near the selected anchor can raise confidence that autumn change is underway, but sparse volunteer records never become a landscape percentage.

3. Weather and drought explain quality risk

NWS hard-freeze and wind signals plus the current U.S. Drought Monitor category are shown separately because leaf drop and stress color can make the woods look different from the historical timing curve.

4. Road closures can override the trip

The tool checks the NPS road-status page and deprioritizes a selected anchor only when a closure range can be clearly parsed. No parsed closure does not mean the road is guaranteed open.

NPS context: The National Park Service notes that the Parkway runs nearly 470 miles and varies by more than 5,000 feet in elevation, so there is no single exact peak date. Higher elevations can begin changing in late September, with the broader progression typically moving through October. Read the NPS fall color guidance.
Before you leave: Check official Blue Ridge Parkway road status. Weather, construction and storm recovery can close sections even when foliage timing is favorable.

Blue Ridge fall color questions

When is peak color on the Blue Ridge Parkway?

There is no single honest peak date for all 469 miles. The useful question is which corridor and elevation band is best matched to the transition now, then how that changes over the next week.

Why can Asheville look green while nearby ridges are turning?

Elevation can create a large temperature difference over a short drive. High ridges can enter the transition while lower valleys are still earlier in the season.

Is this a live color camera or percent-peak map?

No. It is a decision engine built from historical satellite phenology plus separately labeled current evidence. It will not invent a precision the public data cannot support.

What should I use for a weekend drive?

Start with the current and +7-day corridor picks here, then verify NPS road status and local weather before committing to a specific milepost.
